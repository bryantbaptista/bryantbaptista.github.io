## Asking Questions as a Software Engineers

As a current university student, I find myself getting stuck on problems all the time. After reading Eric Raymond's [*How to Ask Questions The Smart Way*](http://www.catb.org/esr/faqs/smart-questions.html), I learned the importance of asking a proper, or "smart" question when you are stuck on a problem. It is always a good idea to do your own research on the problem before you ask others, and if you still can't find the solution, provide as much information and context as possible in order to receive a proper answer.
